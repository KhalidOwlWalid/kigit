Hover a line to see its parts on the board; click it to keep them lit. Edits are drafts, saved as you type in %%DRAFTS_FILE%%, until you write them into the schematic. kigit only writes part information fields (part numbers, manufacturer, distributors, fields you add): never values, footprints, the board or the wiring, and it checks the schematic is the same design before saving. The search links only open the distributors' sites; kigit itself only contacts Mouser and Digi-Key when you press Look up.
